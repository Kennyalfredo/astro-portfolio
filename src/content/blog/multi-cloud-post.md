## About the MCRTA Certification

I am excited to share that I've earned the Multi-Cloud Red Teaming Analyst (MCRTA) certification by Cyberwarfare Labs. This certification focuses on hands-on offensive security across the major cloud platforms: AWS, Azure, and GCP. The course is 100% practical, teaching red team tactics, misconfiguration exploitation, service enumeration, and cloud-specific attack chains in realistic lab scenarios.

## Exam Experience & Preparation

The MCRTA credential is obtained by completing a series of flag-based practical challenges in a multi-cloud lab environment. Each lab pushes you to enumerate services, gain initial access, pivot across accounts, and exfiltrate cloud data using real attack paths. I learned to exploit issues in buckets, containers, IAM roles, VMs, storage permissions, and more. The content covers key techniques like:

- Reconnaissance and Initial Access in AWS, Azure, GCP.
- Credential harvesting, privilege escalation, data exfiltration.
- Automated red teaming operations using various tools.
- Hands-on CTF-style labs with unlimited attempts.
- Exploiting SSRF, RCE, OSINT and misconfigurations.

### What I Used to Prepare

- Cyberwarfare Labs course PDF (150+ pages), video lectures, and the active Discord community.
- Practicing flag-based challenges for each cloud provider (AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Reviewing attack chains, cloud IAM, service enumeration, and red team methodologies.
- Real-world operator experiences shared by the alumni and professional red teamers in the community.

## Who Should Take the MCRTA?

This certification is an excellent entry point for anyone interested in cloud red teaming and penetration testing. It is ideal for professionals with a basic understanding of cloud platforms, and those wanting to expand their skills into multi-cloud environments and adversarial simulation.

## Why It’s Worth It

- Affordable, especially compared to similar practical certifications ($49–$99 range as of 2025)
- Purely hands-on and self-paced—a refreshing alternative to theory-heavy exams
- Direct access to a multi-cloud lab environment; learn by doing
- Builds practical skills for attacking, defending, and assessing AWS, Azure, and GCP
- Recognized by employers looking for cloud security operators and red teamers.

## My Takeaways & Next Steps

After finishing MCRTA, I feel confident performing adversarial operations in cloud environments—a critical skill for any modern red teamer. The certification also opened doors to advanced cloud labs and a global network of practitioners.
