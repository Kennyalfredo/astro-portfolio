### Introduction

In this post, I want to share my journey preparing for and successfully achieving the **eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester (eJPT)** certification. This certification is a great milestone for anyone starting a career in penetration testing and cybersecurity.

### Why I Chose eJPT

- Practical, hands-on exam focus emphasizing real-world skills
- Comprehensive coverage of penetration testing basics
- Highly recognized by employers and cybersecurity professionals

### Preparation Strategy

I followed a structured plan to cover all exam objectives:

- Thoroughly studied the official course materials and labs
- Practiced vulnerable machines and systems in a lab environment
- Used additional resources like forums, videos, and practice tests
- Maintained consistent daily study habits over several weeks

### Exam Experience

The exam involved practical tasks requiring you to apply penetration testing techniques in a controlled environment. It was challenging but rewarding:

- Realistic penetration testing scenarios
- Time management was crucial to complete all exercises
- Demonstrated skills in reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, and reporting

### Key Takeaways

- Master hands-on skills rather than just theory
- Understand common vulnerabilities and exploitation methods
- Develop documentation and reporting abilities
- Build confidence for real client engagements and red team exercises

### Recommendations for Future Candidates

- Practice on labs and vulnerable machines regularly
- Join study groups or communities (Discord, Reddit, etc.)
- Don't rush—focus on fully understanding concepts
- Try to simulate real scenarios beyond course labs
